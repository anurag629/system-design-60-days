# Day 59 posts: LinkedIn and X

The angle is the honesty of revising what you can't explain, not what you already know. Swap in your own voice.

## LinkedIn

Day 59 of 60 days of system design. The least glamorous day, and one of the most useful. No new material. I just rated myself honestly across all eight weeks and spent the day on the three topics that were still shaky.

Here is the trap of a revision day: rereading. You open an old page, the words look familiar, you nod, you feel ready, and you have learned nothing. Recognising is not knowing.

So I did the opposite. For each topic I closed the page and tried to explain it out loud, to an imaginary beginner, from memory. Explain why a B-tree hates random writes. Explain why p99 matters more than the average. Explain fan-out on write versus read. Where my explanation stumbled, where I reached for a word and it was not there, that was a real gap, and that is where the day went.

This is why every day of this course has ended with writing a post. Explaining a thing to strangers is the fastest way to find the hole in your understanding. I have been doing it for 59 days, and today I did it one more time, deliberately, on my weakest spots.

The honest grid matters because a 3 out of 5, "I sort of get it," is a gap, not a pass. An interviewer does not accept hand-waving, and neither should you when grading yourself.

Three things I could not explain cleanly this morning, I can explain tonight. That is the only measure of a review day that counts.
